Who We Are

CCI is an Africa focused business process outsourcing organization catering to a large portfolio of international clients from our delivery locations across the continent. Our emphasis on growth, quality service delivery and commitment to establishing long term partnerships has enabled us to become the top tier BPO operator in South Africa. CCI is transforming how we do business by being impact sourcing pioneers while retaining the essence of what makes us competitive, flexible and adaptable in a dynamic global business environment.

We are a diverse organization that operates from 7 locations across South Africa. Our focus has been on developing people combined with our client centric operating model creating a business enhancing, multichannel, multilingual delivery center capability servicing a global customer base 24×7×365. Our team’s diversity and socially conscious operating model have created a truly unique value proposition within the BPO space.

Through CareerBox (Impact Sourcing Innovators) we provide unique training and work opportunities for highly driven skilled young people who come from disadvantaged backgrounds. CCI South Africa helps set them up for future success as well as helping them make an immediate positive impact on their families and the societies they live in.

CareerBox has impacted over 200,000 lives and has a target of changing an additional 150,000 lives by 2025, impacting over 350,000 lives. CCI South Africa not only invest in communities through CareerBox, but we are also enhancing our shared capability and ensuring skill integration by creating community value in the markets we operate in. We are well versed within the sector, with a vast array of experience that spans across service categories, understanding niche requirements and bringing you cross sector expertise and benefits.

People. Purpose. Progress.

CCI South Africa is a people-first business process outsourcing partner, connecting global brands with exceptional customer experiences while creating meaningful opportunities in our communities.
